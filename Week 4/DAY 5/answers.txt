QUERY: What is the difference between SS304 and SS316?

ANSWER 1 (Assistant):
Here's the difference between SS304 and SS316 stainless steel:

1. **Composition:** Both are austenitic stainless steels with similar iron, chromium (about 18%), and nickel (about 8-10%). SS316 adds about 2-3% molybdenum, which SS304 lacks.

2. **Corrosion resistance:** SS316 resists corrosion better, especially against chlorides such as salt water, de-icing salts, and seawater. Molybdenum is the key reason.

3. **Common uses:**
   - SS304: kitchen sinks, cookware, food equipment, appliances, and general indoor use.
   - SS316: marine hardware, coastal buildings, chemical processing, pharmaceutical equipment, and surgical tools.

4. **Cost:** SS316 costs more, usually about 15-30% more, because of the molybdenum.

**Quick rule:** Choose SS304 for everyday indoor or freshwater use. Choose SS316 for salty, chemical, or harsh environments.

If you have a specific application in mind, let me know and I can help you decide.
